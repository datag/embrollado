## Modification

The font file included here, `../atkinson-next-var-latin.woff2`, is a **subset** of the upstream
variable font:

- Variable axes kept: optical size `opsz` and weight `wght` (200–800).
- Character coverage subset to **Basic Latin**, **Latin-1 Supplement** and **Latin Extended-A**
  (which covers the accented letters of the target languages, including Polish and Turkish),
  plus the punctuation and symbols used by the app and the combining marks referenced by them.
- Converted to `woff2` format.

No glyph outlines were altered; the subset only drops uncovered codepoints. This modification is
disclosed here as required by the OFL, and is visible on the About screen of the app.
